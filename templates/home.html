THE PLATFORM

Every people process. One connected experience.

NEXORA brings key HR workflows into a consistent workspace. Move from employee records to recruitment, onboarding, time management and workforce reporting.

Workforce Management

Maintain employee profiles, department assignments, designations and workforce status in one directory.

Recruitment & Talent

Create job openings, register candidates and follow applications through the hiring pipeline.

Employee Onboarding

Organise induction activities, assign checklist tasks and monitor onboarding progress.

Leave & Attendance

Manage leave requests, approvals, daily attendance entries and employee time records.

Payroll Information

Maintain monthly salary records, allowances, deductions and payment statuses.

Reports & Analytics

Explore workforce distribution, leave activity, attendance records and payroll summaries.

Employee Self-Service

Give employees a dedicated space to review their profile, submit leave requests and view their records.

Access Management

Manage user accounts and assign application roles for HR Admin, Manager and Employee workspaces.

Organisation Structure

View department-level workforce distribution and employee-to-department assignments.

BUILT AROUND CLARITY

Enterprise workflows. Human-centred design.

NEXORA explores how complex HR processes can be presented through a consistent, role-aware and user-focused interface.

Its design direction takes inspiration from modern enterprise applications and SAP Fiori design principles, while remaining an independent HR software simulation.

Unified workspace

Related HR operations are organised into connected modules and consistent screens.

Role-aware experiences

Separate HR and employee experiences with server-side role restrictions.

Structured data

Business records are maintained through a relational MySQL database.

Responsive interface

A consistent experience across desktop, tablet and mobile screen sizes.
